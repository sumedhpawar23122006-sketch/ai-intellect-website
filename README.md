# AI Intellect Launchpad (51)

Prompt:
Build a one-page website for a digital marketing agency called "AI Intellect." The agency provides SEO, branding, website design & development, social media marketing, content marketing, and paid advertising (PPC) services.
Structure the page with these sections:
Hero — Agency name "AI Intellect" and a bold tagline about driving business growth through digital strategy. No CTA button.
Services — A grid/card layout showing each service (SEO, Branding, Web Design & Development, Social Media Marketing, Content Marketing, Paid Advertising) with a short 1-line description and icon for each.
Why Choose Us — 3-4 short points highlighting strengths (data-driven strategy, experienced team, custom solutions).
About — A brief paragraph on the agency's mission to help businesses grow their digital presence.
Contact section — Display only the username "ai.intellect_" as the way to reach out (e.g., "Reach us @ai.intellect_" — styled like a social handle, no form, no email, no phone).
Footer — Logo, "@ai.intellect_" handle, copyright.
Design direction: modern, professional, tech-forward look with a dark navy or charcoal background with galaxy theme, a vibrant accent color (electric blue or violet), clean sans-serif typography, and subtle animations on scroll. Make it fully responsive.
